# AsiBackbone Support and Question Routing

AsiBackbone projects are community-maintained open-source resources. Support is provided on a best-effort basis without a service-level agreement or guaranteed response time.

## Questions, Support, and Proposals

Use [AsiBackbone organization Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/AsiBackbone/discussions) for public questions and open-ended proposals across every AsiBackbone project. State the affected repository and version when the topic is project-specific:

- [Q&A](https://github.com/orgs/AsiBackbone/discussions/categories/q-a) for setup, usage, tutorial, and project-specific questions;
- [Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/AsiBackbone/discussions/categories/ideas) for proposals; and
- [Architecture & Integration](https://github.com/orgs/AsiBackbone/discussions/categories/architecture-integration) for design, tradeoff, and integration discussion.

Use the affected repository's issue tracker for a reproducible defect or clearly scoped work item. Read its support and contribution guidance first because repository-specific policies are authoritative.

## Security and Sensitive Information

Do not disclose passwords, tokens, personal data, exploit details, or other sensitive material in public issues or Discussions. Follow the affected repository's private process described in the [security reporting guidance](SECURITY.md).

These projects provide practical engineering resources. They do not provide private consulting, production incident response, legal advice, compliance certification, or automatic security assurance.
